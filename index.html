Strong Legs & Core Plan

Full-body strength sessions built around your legs, core and back. Every exercise has a gym and a home version, form cues, and a demo video search. Log your weights each session and the page shows your progress.

Get a quick OK from your doctor before starting, especially with any blood pressure, joint or heart concerns. Stop any exercise that causes sharp pain.

Install on your iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app and works without internet.

What moves the needle at 50+

Lift heavy enoughPick a weight where the last 2 reps of each set are hard but your form holds. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, go up 2.5–5 lbs next time.
Protein at every mealMuscle building gets harder after menopause. Aim for a palm-to-hand-sized portion of protein at each meal, and ask your doctor or a dietitian for a daily target.
Brace before you moveTake a breath into your belly, tighten your midsection like you're about to be poked, then lift. This protects your back on every exercise.
Walk on off daysDaily walking does more for steady weight loss than extra hard workouts, and it won't eat into recovery. Sleep 7+ hours when you can.
